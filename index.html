What Timberseek does

Three jobs the data does for your sales team.

No tags, no fluff. The product is a prioritized list of builders starting projects in your territory, ranked by how likely they are to be buying framing material now.

01 — Intelligence

Live permit coverage.

We pull from 48 county and city permit portals across 20 states. New homes, additions, and remodels. Each run reports only the permits you haven't seen before.

02 — Prioritization

Scored on what the permit says.

Four signals, all from the permit record: how far along the job is, how much lumber the work uses (new construction, remodel or outdoor), how many jobs the builder has going, and whether contact details are on file. Scores run from 0 to 50.

03 — Timing

Follow the job after the permit.

Every source's permit status is mapped to the same stages (submitted, issued, inspection, final), so a builder's jobs read the same in any market, and newly issued jobs rank first.

How it works

From public record to warm lead in three steps.

1

Pull

We query county and city permit portals on ArcGIS, Socrata, CKAN, and OpenDataSoft, and normalize every record to one format: contractor, job address, permit type, and status.

2

Score

Each permit is scored from 0 to 50 on its stage, lumber tier, builder activity and contact details. A project's sub-permits count once, and trade-only permits are set aside. Email and phone come straight from the permit, where the city or county publishes them.

3

Deliver

Every Monday: an email summary and a prioritized lead list in CSV, grouped by how you can reach each builder (email and phone, email only, or phone only), plus a market report that ranks your markets and the builders in them.
