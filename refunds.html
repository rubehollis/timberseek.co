Refund Policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · Timberseek LLC

The short version

  • Your first 30 days are free, and no card is needed to start.
  • If your first paid month doesn't earn its keep, email us within 30 days of that charge for a full refund. No questions asked.
  • After that, cancel any time to stop future charges. We don't refund partial months.

1. Free trial

The Builder Feed starts with a 30-day free trial. You don't need to give us a card to start. If you don't add a card by the end of the trial, the trial ends and you're never charged.

2. First paid month: full refund

If you decide the Builder Feed isn't worth it after your first paid month, we'll refund that first month in full.

  • How to ask: email hello@timberseek.co from your account email, or call 678.549.8320, within 30 days of your first charge. You don't have to give a reason.
  • What's refunded: the full amount of your first monthly charge for each market, $499 per market.
  • Quote Desk: the same applies to your first paid month of the Builder Quote Desk add-on, if you choose to keep it after its free pilot.
  • When you'll see it: we process approved refunds within 2 business days. It usually takes 5 to 10 business days for the money to show up on your card, depending on your bank.
  • Your subscription: when we refund your first month, we also cancel that subscription so you aren't charged again.
  • The first-month refund is available once per company per market.

3. Later months

After your first paid month, monthly charges aren't refundable. You can cancel any time, and you won't be charged again. Your Service continues until the end of the month you've already paid for.

4. Exceptions where we always refund

  • You were charged by mistake, charged twice, or charged after you cancelled.
  • We failed to deliver your feed for two or more weeks in a billing month because of a problem on our end. We'll refund that month in proportion to the weeks missed.
  • We end your account without cause, or remove a core feature you're paying for. We'll refund the unused part of your current month.

5. How to cancel

Use the billing link in your account emails, or email hello@timberseek.co. We'll confirm your cancellation by email.

6. Questions or disputes

If something about a charge looks wrong, please contact us before disputing it with your bank. We reply within three business days and will make it right.
