Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026 · Timberseek LLC

The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run your trial and subscription.
  • We never sell your information or add you to marketing lists.
  • Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Builder contact details in the feed come from public permit records. Builders can ask us to remove theirs.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Timberseek LLC ("Timberseek," "we," "us") collects and uses information through timberseek.co and the Timberseek service (the "Service"). It covers two groups of people: our customers and website visitors, and the builders and contractors whose details appear in public permit records.

2. Information we collect from customers

  • What you send us when you sign up: your name, work email, company, phone number, the markets you deliver to, your business type, your yard or plant address, delivery radius, and the jobs you supply.
  • Your builder accounts: the list of current builder customers you send us for Account Watch.
  • Billing information: when you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment details. We receive limited information from Stripe, such as your billing name, the last four digits of your card, and payment status.
  • Emails and messages: anything you write to us, and records of our replies.

3. How we use customer information

  • To set up your trial and build your feed for your market and delivery radius.
  • To keep your own builder accounts out of your "new builders" list and track them in Account Watch.
  • To bill you, send receipts, and handle refunds.
  • To answer your questions and send service messages, such as your Monday feed, your day-20 trial recap, and notices about billing or changes to the Service.
  • To improve the Service, for example by learning which reports customers find useful.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect against fraud.

We don't sell your information, rent it, or put you on marketing lists. Your builder account list is never shared with other customers.

4. Builder and contractor information from public records

The feed includes information that cities and counties publish in construction permit records, such as a builder's or contractor's company name, license number, job address, and the email and phone number listed on the permit. We collect this from official public data portals and share it with subscribing building-material suppliers so they can offer their products.

If you're a builder or contractor and want your contact details removed from future Timberseek feeds, email hello@timberseek.co with your name or company and the email or phone to remove. We'll remove them from future feeds within 10 business days. This doesn't change the public record itself, which is kept by the permitting office.

5. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Stripe, for payment processing.
  • Microsoft 365, for email.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the fonts on this website and receives your browser's IP address when the page loads.
  • Cloud hosting and data tools we use to build and deliver the feed.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or others' safety, or as part of a sale or merger of the business, in which case this policy would continue to apply.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. If we add analytics in the future, we'll update this policy first.

7. How long we keep information

We keep customer information while your account is active. After you cancel, we delete your setup details and builder account list within 90 days, unless you ask us to keep them so you can restart easily. We keep billing records as long as tax and accounting laws require, generally seven years.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, including encrypted connections, access controls, and limiting access to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but if we learn of a breach affecting your information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

9. Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we hold about you by emailing hello@timberseek.co. We'll respond within 30 days. Service emails, such as your feed and billing notices, stop when you cancel.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and isn't directed to children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll post the new policy here and email current customers before it takes effect.
